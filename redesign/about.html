Our story

Citizen science, built for the data gaps

sensors.AFRICA is a citizen-science project by Code for Africa that addresses the data gaps across the continent by providing low-cost sensors that people use to measure and monitor the air, water and sound quality of their own communities.

Our air quality sensors are built on open-source technology from the Luftdaten project — hardware cheap enough for a household to host, and open enough for anyone to inspect. The initiative was seed-funded by innovateAFRICA and is incubated by Code for Africa.

Citizens and governments alike are increasingly aware of the toxic health risks of air pollution — but what they lack is accessible, hyper-local, real-time data about the air on their own streets. sensors.AFRICA changes this. Data from our low-cost sensor network is already being used by watchdog NGOs and journalists to spotlight public-health risks: live readings for what is happening right now, and historical archives for the patterns that only emerge over months and years.
